# ARVEXA Literature Sources

This directory stores the structured literature evidence used by ARVEXA's research planning.

## Files

### `literature-database.xlsx`

This is the structured bibliography supplied for the ARVEXA literature review.

It contains the curated paper list and project-specific analysis fields, including:

- paper number,
- sub-area,
- title,
- authors,
- year,
- venue,
- reported citations,
- source,
- URL,
- relevance note,
- implementable research gap,
- gap importance.

The supplied survey reports **113 curated papers**.

## How it is used

The database supports:

```text
Literature Evidence
       ↓
Research Gap
       ↓
Research Questions
       ↓
Requirements
       ↓
Architecture
       ↓
Experiments
       ↓
Evaluation
```

The Markdown research documents provide the narrative interpretation, while this workbook preserves the structured evidence and source metadata.

## Copyright and paper PDFs

Do **not** place downloaded copyrighted paper PDFs in this directory by default.

The repository should store bibliographic metadata, links, notes, and research analysis. Individual PDFs should only be added when the team has confirmed that redistribution in the repository is permitted, such as for appropriately licensed/open-access material.

## Maintenance rules

When adding a paper:

1. Preserve the original title and author metadata.
2. Record the publication year and venue.
3. Record the source used to locate the paper.
4. Keep the official URL or DOI where available.
5. Record why the paper is relevant to ARVEXA.
6. Record the specific research gap or limitation relevant to ARVEXA.
7. Distinguish peer-reviewed publications from preprints.
8. Avoid treating citation counts as permanent; record the date/source when updating them.
